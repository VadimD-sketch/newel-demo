Wallbox and charging post installation in your association

Whether you have a garage, open parking or a shared facility, we help you from first need to working charging.

Wallbox in the garage

We install wallboxes at the spaces in the association's garage. We review the electrical installation and propose a solution that suits you.

Charging post for the association

Charging posts on shared parking for homeowner associations and joint facilities, with billing per user.

The cost follows the user

With IMD (individual metering and billing) each user is billed for their actual consumption, so the association never pays for someone else's charging.
