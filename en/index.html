From quote request to installation in five steps

This is how it works when you install wallboxes or charging posts with NEWEL.

  1. 1

    Quote request

    You tell us about the site and how many charge points you want.

  2. 2

    Review

    We go through the electrical installation, the parking and your wishes.

  3. 3

    Quote

    You get a clear quote to take to the board or management.

  4. 4

    Installation

    Our installers mount the wallboxes or charging posts at your site.

  5. 5

    Start-up and support

    Charging goes live, IMD is connected where needed, and you have support to turn to.
